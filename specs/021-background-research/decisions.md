No copy of the facts. Rejected: O34 (copy findings into `research.md`, facts in two places).
- D8 (Q9): O35. At most 3 grill research agents run at a time. A fourth scope goes into a queue, and the orchestrator starts it when one of the 3 ends. Rejected: O36 (skip it and leave it to Plan).
- D9 (Q10): O37, changed by the user. Before the spec gate, Define waits for every research agent that runs and shows each finding that changes the spec. The gate names research that stopped or failed. Scopes still in the queue do not start and are not waited for: Define passes them to Plan, and Plan runs them through `/stratum:st-research` (into `research/`) before the `st-plan` agent starts. Rejected: O38 (wait only for spec research, Plan-only research runs on past the gate).
- D10 (Q11): O39. Background research applies only to the grill inside Define (the issue title scopes it to Define). The bar stays: a quick look first, and research starts only for a fact the quick look cannot find. It now covers facts the plan will need too, not only facts a grill question needs. A grill run alone (`/stratum:st-grill`) keeps today's rule with the OK step. Rejected: O40 (every grill).
